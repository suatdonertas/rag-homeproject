About the job
Job Description

How do you help make a difference?

As a Technical Architect within Integration Services of Infrastructure team, you play a key role in shaping and evolving the organization’s integration landscape. You are responsible for driving the technical vision, governance, and continuous improvement of integration technologies, ensuring they meet current and future business needs.

You work across a diverse ecosystem of integration platforms, including message brokers, event streaming, API management, and managed file transfer solutions (e.g., MQ, Kafka, Axway, AMQP Broker, API Platform).

Reporting to the Team lead of Integration Services

What is your role at SNCB?

As a Technical Architect you will take on the following key responsibilities:

Architecture & Strategy

Define and maintain the target architecture for Integration Services.
Design and evolve integration building blocks to support business projects.
Maintain and update technology roadmaps for both existing and emerging integration solutions.
Ensure alignment with enterprise architecture standards and long-term IT strategy.

Technology Evolution & Innovation

Research, evaluate, and recommend new integration technologies and patterns.
Support the evolution of existing platforms (e.g., performance, scalability, security improvements).
Drive adoption of modern integration paradigms such as event-driven architecture and API-first design.

Governance & Processes

Contribute to and improve internal processes and standards within Integration Services.

Delivery Support

Support project teams in selecting and implementing appropriate integration solutions.
Collaborate with development, operations, and security teams to ensure robust solutions.

Continuous Improvement

Identify opportunities to optimize integration services and reduce complexity.
Enhance reusability and standardization of integration components.
Monitor industry trends and incorporate relevant innovations.

Job Requirements

Why is this job a good fit for you?

Technical Expertise

At least 5 years of relevant experience as Technical Architect
Strong experience with integration technologies such as:
Messaging systems (e.g., MQ, AMQP)
Event streaming platforms (e.g., Kafka)
API management platforms (e.g., Axway Amplify)
Managed file transfer tools (e.g., Sterling)
Solid understanding of:
Integration patterns (request/reply, pub/sub, event-driven)
Microservices and distributed systems
Security protocols (OAuth, TLS, etc.)
Experience with cloud and hybrid integration architectures is a plus
Experience in Infrastructure as Code and Kubernetes is a plus
Architecture & Design

Proven experience in solution and/or enterprise architecture
Ability to design scalable, resilient, and maintainable integration solutions
Familiarity with architecture frameworks and modeling techniques

Soft Skills

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Ability to influence and guide stakeholders across teams
Excellent communication and documentation skills
Proactive mindset with a focus on continuous improvement
Good knowledge of English and Dutch or French (preferably with at least a passive knowledge of the second language)
